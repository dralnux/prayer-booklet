Novena for the Departed Soul

This booklet presents a single prayer sequence. Pray the complete sequence each day for nine consecutive days.

the deceased

Prayer for the Departed Soul

Come to his/her aid O saints of God; come forth to meet him/her.
Angels of the Lord receiving his/her soul presenting it to the Most High.
May Christ who has called you, now receive you and may the angels bring you to Abraham’s bosom. Receiving his/her soul presenting it to the most high, eternal rest grant unto him/her O, Lord and let perpetual light shine upon him/her.

O Lord, we recommend to you the soul of your servant _____ (deceased name), that having departed from this world he/she may live with you. By the grace of your mercy and love, wash away the sins that in human frailty he/she has committed in the conduct of his/her life through Christ our Lord. Amen

Leader: Eternal rest grant unto him/her, O Lord
All: And let perpetual light shine upon him/her

Leader: From the gates of hell
All: Deliver his/her soul

Leader: May he/she rest in peace
All: Amen

The Apostle’s Creed (new version)

I believe in God, the Father almighty,
Creator of Heaven and earth
and in Jesus Christ, His only Son, our Lord,
who was conceived by the Holy Spirit,
born of the Virgin Mary,
suffered under Pontius Pilate,
was crucified, died and was buried;
He descended into hell;
on the third day He rose again from the dead;
He ascended into Heaven
and is seated at the right hand of God the Father almighty;
from there He will come to judge the living and the dead.
I believe in the Holy Spirit, the Holy Catholic Church, the Communion of Saints,
the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and life everlasting.
Amen.

Let Us Pray

O God the Creator and Redeemer of all the faithful, grant unto the soul of your servant departed the remissions of all his/her sins, so that by pious supplications he/she may obtain the pardon he/she has always desired. Grant this O Lord, who lives and reigns for ever and ever. Amen

O Eternal God, who besides the general precept of charity has commanded a particular respect to parents, kindred, benefactors and friends, grant we beseech you, that they were the instruments by which your Providence bestowed on us our birth, education and innumerable other blessings, so our prayers may be the means to obtain for him/her a speedy release from his/her excessive sufferings and free admittance to your infinite joys, through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen

O God, whose property is ever to have mercy and to spare, we humbly beseech you on behalf of your servant _____ (deceased name) whom you called out of this world that you would not deliver him/her into the hands of your enemies nor forget him/her forever but bid the holy angels to lead him/her into Paradise his/her true country. So that as much as in you he/she puts his/her hope and trust, he/she may not endure the pains of hell but may come to the possession of eternal joy, through Jesus Christ who lives and reigns in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God world without end. Amen
