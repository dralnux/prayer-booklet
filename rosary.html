Rosary Prayers

Opening: Sign of the Cross, Apostles' Creed, Our Father, three Hail Marys, Glory Be.

Each decade: Our Father, ten Hail Marys, Glory Be, followed by the Fatima Prayer if desired.

Conclusion: Hail Holy Queen and the Sign of the Cross.
