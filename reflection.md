### 2026-03-01 — Becoming Forgetful?

**Scripture:** Genesis 12:2; Matthew 17:1-9

**Author:** Bo Sanchez

**REFLECT:**
> Bo Sanchez lists four forms of spiritual dementia: forgetting one's identity as God's child, one's home in God's family, one's purpose beyond self-interest, and that everything belongs to God. He concludes, 'But even if you forget, God always remembers.'
