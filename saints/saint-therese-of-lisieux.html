Saint Therese of Lisieux

The Little Flower, Doctor of the Church

Prayer to Saint Therese of Lisieux

St. Therese, the little flower, please pick a rose from the heavenly garden and send it to me with a message of love. Ask God to grant me the favor I ask and tell Him I will love Him each day more and more.

Amen.
