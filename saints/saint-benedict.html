Prayers to the Saints

Saint Benedict

Father of Western Monasticism and patron of a holy death

Prayer to Saint Benedict

Gracious and loving God, we thank you for the life and example of Saint Benedict. Through his prayers, help us to seek you above all things, to resist temptation, and to grow in faith, hope, and love. May his intercession protect us from evil and guide us closer to Christ. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.
